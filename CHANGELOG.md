## English
### v2.4.4
- Added: Ctrl-C can also open the copy menu
- Updated: Bumped to 26.3

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Minecraft changed the meaning of keycodes when updating from 26.2 to 26.3.
> Keycodes in previously saved configurations may now **map to completely different keys**.
> If you need to reuse configurations created in older versions of Minecraft, please check them carefully!
